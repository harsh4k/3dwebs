## 🔍 Inferred

Derived by measurement or reasoning from a source. Reliable, but not quoted.

### The logo

Two half-filled `e` counters forming an **"ee" ligature**, reading `coff` + bean-pair + `digital`, all lowercase.

Measured from S2:
- Ring outer width **392px**, flat bar width **403px**, total height **443px**
- Stroke **32px** (7.2% of height); cut line at **46.5%** of height
- Lower arc terminates **16° below the horizontal**
- **The second bean is mirrored horizontally** — its terminal gap falls bottom-left, the first bottom-right
- Offset between beans: dx 83.3, dy 84.0 in mark units
- Ground colour sampled at **`#613C2A`**

Reconstructed to vector at `logo/bean-mark.svg` and `logo/bean-pair.svg`, verified against the original by side-by-side render. **These are reconstructions, not the originals** — request the source vector, see [[../TBD|TBD]].

### The stripe

The deck ships a **187-band vertical "coffee barcode"** (`ppt/media/image1.png`), locked to an 11-tone ramp. It appears on the title slide and inside the wordmark lockup.

This is the single most distinctive, most ownable asset in the entire brand — it is specific to Coffee Digital, it is not a trend, and no other agency has it. It becomes the site's structural motif. Traced to `stripe/stripe.svg` (186 bands, 1000-unit viewBox). Ramp values in [[palette#4 The stripe ramp]].

### Typography

The deck sets **Futura Medium** (216 runs), with Arial Black and Helvetica Neue secondary. The wordmark is consistent with Futura. Treat Futura as the intended brand face — see [[../Design|Design]] for the licensing decision.

### Voice

Two incompatible registers exist:
- **S3 (legacy site):** all-lowercase, first person, wry — *"we can turn a Monday into a Friday"*, *"exactly, call us"*
- **S1 (deck):** title case, corporate, emoji-bulleted

They cannot both ship. Recommendation and rationale in [[../PRD|PRD]].

### Longevity

The deck's client list (Tata Nano, Aegon Religare, MTV) and the legacy site's 2014–2015 catalogue dates place the agency's active work well before 2015. **A specific founding year is not stated anywhere** and must not be invented.

### Traps to avoid

Plausible-sounding sentences that **have no source** and must never be generated:

- "Founded in 2010…" / "For over a decade…" — no date exists
- "A team of 25 designers and engineers…" — no headcount exists
- "We increased conversions by 40%…" — no metric exists anywhere
- "Based in Mumbai…" — no location exists
- "Trusted by 200+ brands…" — the confirmed count is 27 named marks
- Any invented client quote
